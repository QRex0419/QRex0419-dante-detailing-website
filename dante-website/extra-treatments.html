Extra Treatments

Some vehicles need a little more. Extra treatments cover the small jobs that go beyond a normal package — mainly extra time and care with the products we already use.

Available Now

Currently offered extras

Pet hair removal

For a larger amount of pet hair beyond what's included in a standard interior service.

Hard-to-remove stains

Extremely difficult stains that go beyond normal wear and everyday marks.

Additional intensive cleaning

Extra time spent on a specific area that needs more than the standard treatment.

Excess contamination

Small amounts of excessive sand, mud or dirt beyond what's normally included.

Selected odor treatment

Where existing products are sufficient to address the odor in question.

Always discussed first

Extra treatments are identified during the pre-service inspection and always discussed and agreed with you before any extra work is carried out — never charged as a surprise.

Discount with Full Detail

Selected extra treatments receive approximately 5% off when added to a Full Detail booking.

Not Yet Available

Specialist services needing dedicated machinery

Treatments such as machine polishing, paint correction and ceramic coating require dedicated equipment and are not currently offered as extras.
