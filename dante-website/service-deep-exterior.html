What's included

Everything from Exterior Detail, plus:

  • Chemical paint decontamination, when necessary
  • Iron contamination removal, when necessary
  • Tar removal, when necessary
  • Paint inspection
  • More extensive paint preparation
  • Longer-lasting paint protection

Mechanical clay, when necessary

Mechanical clay treatment is used only when the paint needs it, and is treated as an additional treatment rather than something automatically included.

Not included in Deep Exterior

Machine polishing and paint correction are not part of Deep Exterior Detail. These advanced treatments are coming soon.
