The Process

How It Works

A simple, transparent process — from your booking request to the moment you get your keys back.

1

Book an appointment

Send us a request with your vehicle, desired service and preferred date. We confirm your appointment personally.

2

Vehicle inspection

Before we start, we inspect the vehicle together with you and assess its condition.

3

Discuss additional work

If the work needed goes beyond the normal scope of your package, we discuss it first — you always know about additional charges before any extra work begins.

4

Detailing begins

We carry out your chosen package carefully and methodically, at your location.

5

Final inspection

Every treatment ends with a final inspection and finishing touches.

6

Vehicle returned

We walk through the result together and hand your car back — clean, fresh and cared for.

No surprise charges

Unexpected or additional work is always discussed before it's performed — and only charged after your explicit agreement.

Before Your Appointment

Good to know

Personal belongings

To give your vehicle the most thorough treatment — and to avoid any misunderstandings about personal items — we kindly ask you to remove your belongings before the appointment:

  • Trunk / cargo area
  • Centre console
  • Glove compartment
  • Door pockets
  • Other storage spaces

If personal belongings remain in an area, that area may not be treated. Thank you for your understanding.

Inspection & additional work

Every appointment starts with a vehicle inspection, together with you. Minor additional contamination remains included in your package. When the work required is significantly beyond normal scope, we discuss it first — an additional amount is only charged after your agreement.
