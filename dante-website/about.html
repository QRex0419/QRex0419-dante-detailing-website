About Us

DANTE is being built around one idea: care done properly

We're a new Belgian mobile detailing company, currently in preparation for launch. Here's what we stand for.

Our Story

A new company with a clear standard

DANTE starts as a mobile detailing service: we come to you, and we detail your car where it stands — at home, at work, wherever suits you. No fixed workshop, no drop-offs, no waiting around.

We work primarily with private car owners, and we also welcome companies, garages and car dealerships. Every vehicle gets the same careful treatment, whether it's a daily city car or something special.

The full story behind DANTE — who's behind it and why — will be shared here as we get closer to launch.

What We Stand For

Passion for automotive care

This business is being built by people who genuinely care about cars — not as a volume operation, but as a craft.

Attention to detail

Detailing is in the name. The seams, the vents, the door jambs — the places that make the difference.

Quality-focused work

We'd rather do fewer cars properly than many cars quickly. Every appointment gets the time it needs.

Professional service

Clear communication, transparent pricing and agreements you can rely on — before, during and after the job.

Where We're Headed

Mobile detailing is the beginning. Over time we plan to add advanced treatments such as machine polishing and ceramic coating, and grow towards a fixed detailing location. You can follow that journey on our Coming Soon page.
