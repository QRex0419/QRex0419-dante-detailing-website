Interior Detail

A thorough interior refresh for everyday driving — every surface cleaned, every corner reached, finished with a final inspection.

What's included

Interior Detail covers the full cabin, including:

  • Full vacuuming — seats, floors, mats and cargo area
  • Dashboard cleaning
  • Center console cleaning
  • Door panel cleaning
  • Steering wheel and frequently touched areas
  • Hard-to-reach areas
  • Interior glass
  • Mat cleaning
  • Normal interior plastics, vinyl, rubber and similar surfaces
  • Final inspection and finishing

Not automatically included

Special treatments such as excessive pet hair, extreme stains, strong odors and restoration work go beyond the normal scope of Interior Detail. These are available as Extra Treatments, always discussed and agreed with you first.
